How to use AnyThing

Save videos, reels, audio and photos from social media in four taps.

  1. Pick a typeTap Reels, Video, Audio, Photo, Stories, FB Video or Shorts at the top.
  2. Paste the linkCopy the post link in the social app, then tap Paste. You can also paste into the box yourself.
  3. Tap PreviewWait a moment while the media details load.
  4. DownloadTap a format button to save it. Turn on “Start download automatically” to skip this tap next time.

Change the look

  • Tap the round theme button to choose Light, Dark, Rain, Winter or Summer.
  • Your theme is remembered the next time you visit.

If something doesn’t work

  • Refresh the page and try the link again.
  • Private or deleted posts can’t be fetched. Try a public link.
  • Use the full link from the app’s Share menu.

Good to know

Only download content you own or have permission to save.
